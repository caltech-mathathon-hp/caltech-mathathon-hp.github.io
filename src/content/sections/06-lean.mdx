The [Lean project](https://github.com/mathathon-caltech-hp/mathathon-caltech-hp.github.io/tree/lean-certificates/lean/cubic)
contains the cubic problem definitions, geometric proofs, verifiers and reductions.
The final NP-hardness and NP-completeness theorems take an explicit Cook–Levin
hypothesis. Their complete signatures and source locations are in the
[theorem index](https://github.com/mathathon-caltech-hp/mathathon-caltech-hp.github.io/blob/lean-certificates/lean/cubic/docs/theorem-index.md).

## Scope

The project uses Lean 4.24.0 and mathlib v4.24.0. Its NP and Karp-reduction
definitions use mathlib's finite encodings and actual polynomial-time TM2
machines. The verifier and reduction running times are proved in Lean.

The geometric proof uses an alternative cubic frame while preserving the HP
and perfect-folding problem statements and encodings. It is not a line-by-line
formalisation of the paper's Figure 3 construction. The exact model choices,
including counting backbone H–H bonds, are documented in the project README.

Theorem 3.2 and both NP memberships are unconditional. The final complexity
results retain `(hCL : SAT.CookLevin)`; this distribution does not prove that
premise. Axiom checks permit only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
Inspecting the theorem types is also necessary: an axiom check alone does not
remove explicit hypotheses. The project includes build instructions, an import
and hash manifest, and CI that reruns its checks.
