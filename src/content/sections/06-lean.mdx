## Scope

We found no formalized hardness result for HP folding in any proof assistant. The only Lean library
for the HP model, Ptyche by <Cite k="kjoshanssen2024ptyche" />, has the definitions and score
bounds, but no hardness. Mathlib has no notion of NP or of polynomial-time reductions; see
<Cite k="mathlib2026computability" />.

We plan to follow the approach of <Cite k="kreuzer2023verification" />. The definitions and the
correctness of the reduction (*a perfect fold exists if and only if the instance has a
solution*) will be proved in Lean, together with a bound on the length of the string. The
polynomial running time of the reduction will be argued on paper. A statement on this site carries
the label <Status kind="lean" /> only if it passes every check listed above.
